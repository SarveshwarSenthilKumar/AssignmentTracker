Internet-sourced resume layouts

Assignment Tracker provides Word adaptations of the layout ideas in these
LaTeX projects. These are not official Word editions or pixel-identical renders.
Original authors' personal resume data is not included in the generated files.
The original templates and their licenses are linked below. License notices
are retained here and in the generated Word document's description metadata.
